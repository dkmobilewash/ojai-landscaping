WHERE WE WORK

Proudly Serving the Ojai Valley & Surrounding Communities

No matter where you are in the valley, Ojai Valley Landscaping Co. is your local landscaping team.

Ojai

Our home base. We serve residential and commercial properties throughout downtown Ojai and the surrounding neighborhoods.

Meiners Oaks

Lawn care, tree trimming, and landscape design for this charming unincorporated community just west of Ojai.

Upper Ojai

Rural property maintenance, brush clearing, fire clearance, and irrigation services for hillside and ranch-style homes in the upper valley.

Oak View

Full-service landscaping and maintenance for residential properties in Oak View and the communities along Highway 33.

Casitas Springs

Lawn maintenance, cleanup, and hardscape work for properties along the lower valley corridor near Lake Casitas.

Lake Casitas Area

Landscape maintenance and water-wise design for properties near the lake and Casitas Municipal Water District service areas.

Matilija Canyon

Specialty rural and hillside landscaping, erosion control, and native planting for properties in this scenic canyon corridor.

Sulphur Mountain / El Roblar

Property maintenance, tree work, and fire clearance for rural hillside estates and ranches.
